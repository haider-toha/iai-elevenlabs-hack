# Sources — provenance for the demo letter wording

Every authentic string in `p2-verbatim-strings.md` (and the seed migration) traces
to one of the URLs below. All are GOV.UK / official UK government pages under the
**Open Government Licence v3.0** unless noted, so the wording is safe to reuse with
the OGL attribution line in the footer. Fetched 2026-06-25.

| Ref | URL | What it confirms |
|---|---|---|
| S1 | https://www.gov.uk/tax-codes | Canonical clause: *"Your tax code is used by your employer or pension provider to work out how much Income Tax to take from your pay or pension."* Drives the opening paragraph. |
| S2 | https://www.gov.uk/tax-codes/what-your-tax-code-means | Number × 10 = tax-free income (1257 → £12,570); suffix `L` = *"You're entitled to the standard tax-free Personal Allowance."* |
| S3 | https://www.gov.uk/income-tax-rates | Standard Personal Allowance **£12,570** for the tax year 6 April 2026 to 5 April 2027; tax-year phrasing. |
| S4 | https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/paye-manual/paye11040 | The gold source: verbatim standard P2 explanatory notes — Personal Allowance note, company-car note, and the adjustment line *"We have therefore included an adjustment to reduce your tax-free allowance by £<amount> so we can collect the £<amount> tax in equal instalments."* |
| S5 | https://www.gov.uk/tax-codes/letters-about-your-tax-code | Confirms the letter is a "Tax Code Notice"; the "check your information is correct and contact HMRC" footer sentiment. |
| S6 | https://www.gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/paye-manual/paye11030 | P2 / Notice of Coding structure: Personal Allowance entitlement, items reducing the tax-free amount, positive/negative (K-code) tax-free amount, per-element explanatory notes. |
| S7 | https://www.gov.uk/tax-codes/k-in-your-tax-code | Verbatim K-code definition: *"Tax codes with a 'K' mean you have income or deductions which are higher than your tax-free Personal Allowance and are not already being taxed."* |
| S8 | https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/doc/open-government-licence/version/3/ | Verbatim OGL v3.0 attribution: *"Contains public sector information licensed under the Open Government Licence v3.0."* |
| S9 | https://design-system.service.gov.uk/patterns/national-insurance-numbers/ | Verbatim recommended display format `QQ 12 34 56 C` and the instruction to use `QQ…` instead of a real NI number. |
| S10 | https://www.gov.uk/tax-overpayments-and-underpayments | *"tax calculation letter (also known as a P800)"*; sent *"If you've paid too much or too little tax by the end of the tax year (5 April)."* |
| S11 | https://www.gov.uk/tax-overpayments-and-underpayments/if-youre-due-a-refund | Verbatim refund timings: *"5 working days if you've claimed online"*; *"6 weeks if you've asked HMRC to send you a cheque."* |
